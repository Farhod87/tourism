A Brief History of Tashkent

Tashkent is the capital of Uzbekistan and one of the oldest cities in Central Asia, over 2200 years old. The city was an important center on the Great Silk Road.

Throughout its history, Tashkent has survived many eras: from ancient states to the Timurid Empire, from the Kokand Khanate to a modern metropolis. Today it is the largest cultural, scientific, and economic center of the country, where antiquity and modernity harmoniously combine.
