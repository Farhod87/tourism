A Brief History of Bukhara

Bukhara is one of the oldest cities in Central Asia, over 2500 years old. The city was an important center on the Great Silk Road, connecting East and West.

Science, culture, religion, and crafts were formed here, and great scholars and thinkers glorified Bukhara throughout the world. Today, Bukhara is an open-air museum where more than 140 architectural monuments have been preserved since ancient times. The city is included in the UNESCO World Heritage List.
