Tour Description

The UAE is a country of contrasts, where futuristic skyscrapers coexist with desert dunes and luxurious shopping malls with traditional oriental markets. This tour is perfect for those who want to combine a beach holiday on the shores of the Persian Gulf with vivid impressions: from the Burj Khalifa observation deck to a desert safari and walks along Dubai Marina.

Itinerary

Tour Itinerary

Start getting acquainted with Dubai — the city of the future on the shores of the Persian Gulf.

Excursions with a Russian-speaking guide and free time on the beach:

  • Ascent to the Burj Khalifa observation deck — the world's tallest building (828 m).
  • A walk along Dubai Marina — a promenade with skyscrapers, yachts, and restaurants.
  • Safari in the desert — a jeep tour over the dunes, camel riding, and dinner with a show.
  • Excursion to Abu Dhabi — the capital of the UAE, the Sheikh Zayed Mosque and Qasr Al Watan palace.
  • Visit to Dubai Mall — the largest shopping center with an aquarium and a fountain.
  • Beach holiday on Jumeirah Beach with a view of the Burj Al Arab hotel.

Tour duration: 6 days / 5 nights
